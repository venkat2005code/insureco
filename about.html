Our story

From regional insurer to global risk partner.

Founded in 1995, InsurCo began as a focused regional insurer serving local families and small businesses. As our customers’ needs evolved, so did our ambition.

Today, we operate across more than 50 countries, blending disciplined underwriting with real-time data to deliver responsive coverage for individuals, employers, and institutions.

  • Dedicated centers of excellence across health, life, property and casualty.
  • Technology-first claims and policy management built on a secure cloud platform.
  • Local teams in key regions to stay close to customer realities and regulation.
